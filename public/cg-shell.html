Community Sensemaking · The Polis Method

See where we align —
and where we're still growing.

Permaculture is full of living debates — what truly counts, what works, which practices to feature. Members respond to a series of statements, and the Polis method groups those responses by how they pattern together — not simple yes-or-no tallies — mapping where the community shares common ground and where it still sees things differently. The groups describe patterns in the answers, not fixed types of people; every perspective stays visible, none is averaged away, and Mycelium stays neutral on what the field hasn't settled.
