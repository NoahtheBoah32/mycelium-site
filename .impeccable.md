## Design Context

Mycelium is Joaquin's free permaculture learning platform, rooted in the Philippines and open to learners elsewhere. Visitors choose resources, explore research, and join community conversations. Many visit on phones.

The user wants a welcoming, organic, non-corporate site with exceptionally detailed imagery. Approved references include Plantica and Interstellar Lab. Preserve the forest homepage, the exposed roots, gentle plant motion, floating cells, and the full animated sequences. Never trade their visual quality for speed.

The approved article pages use off-white, gold, and brown with editorial serif typography. Preserve their content layout while giving research articles the main site's familiar logo and top-right Menu. Make primary actions obvious without making every action equally prominent.

Use natural invitations in Mycelium's voice. Avoid repetitive slogans, padded copy, and habitual lists of three. Founder story, newsletter, and footer copy are approved. Common Ground's forum UI, functionality, and stored data must remain untouched.
